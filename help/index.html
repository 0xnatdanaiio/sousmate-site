Questions

What records does it keep?

Fridges and chillers, cooking, cooling, deliveries, problems, staff training, equipment and calibration. Hot holding and cleaning too, if you want: those records are optional.

On SousMate, Inspection day turns them into one PDF for your verifier.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Records save on the device. On SousMate they back up when you're online. On Free they stay on the device.

Which devices?

A phone, tablet or PC, in the browser. Nothing to download.

What does it cost?

Free is NZ$0: every record on 1 device, and CSV download, always. No backup, PDF or extra devices.

SousMate is NZ$19.99 a month or NZ$199 a year: backup kept for 4 years, all your devices and the Inspection day PDF.

One price for the whole shop: no charge per staff member.

How do staff use it?

Each person taps their name once per shift, so every record shows who did it. On SousMate, staff on another device join the shop with a code.

Can a record be changed or deleted?

A correction is added and the original stays. Nothing is deleted.

How long must I keep records?

At least 4 years. See Kept safe for what SousMate does with your records.

Which plan do I need, FCP or NP2?

SousMate doesn't decide that. It says which on your registration. Not sure? Ask your council.

Is SousMate approved by MPI?

SousMate is an independent app. It is not part of, or approved by, the Ministry for Primary Industries (MPI) or the New Zealand Government. Check your own food control plan or national programme for what applies to you.

How do I cancel?

Cancelling stops at the end of the period you've paid for. Then your shop moves to Free.
