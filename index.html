What SousMate does

Record as you go

Walk round once: every fridge and freezer is on one screen. Type the °C or tap Still frozen ✓, then save once. It says OK or Too warm in plain words, and keeps who checked it and when.

Know what's due

See what's left to check today, and what's coming up: registration renewal, your verification visit, staff training, recall test and equipment checks on your own schedule. Add each date once and SousMate counts down.

Ready for your verifier

Open Inspection day and hand over one PDF. Page 1 sums up every record type, the days a fridge wasn't checked and any problems, with page numbers for the rest.

Why we built SousMate

I grew up around my mum's food caravan and her takeaway shop here in New Zealand. Every time an inspector came, we scrambled to get the paperwork together: stained pages, damp pages, never quite tidy. Years later, in my family's other shops, it was the same story.

I've been around food businesses since I was a teenager, then spent 12 years in food manufacturing, where every job I had came down to food safety and good hygiene. I built SousMate so every shop can do its checks quickly, serve safe food, and stop keeping boxes of paper for years.
