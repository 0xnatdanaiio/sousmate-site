Cleaning and equipment

Clean. Service.
Calibrate.

  • A cleaning plan in minutes

    Start with suggested tasks, then make them yours: what, how, how often and who.

  • Tick what you cleaned

    One tap per task, or Tick all, then save. A cleaning record is optional, so SousMate never calls a day “missed”.

  • Equipment, maintenance and repairs

    One list of your equipment. Log a service or a repair: what was done, when and who did it.

  • Thermometer and scale checks

    Calibrate in ice slush or boiling water, against another thermometer, or with a test weight. Set your own schedule and What's due shows the next date.

  • In the verifier PDF

    A weekly cleaning page, plus your equipment list, maintenance and calibration.
